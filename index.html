Listing Optimisation System

Concept Demonstration

Before: The Generic Listing

Basic Title: Vague keywords, poor search visibility.

Standard Images: Unedited supplier photos, no lifestyle context.

Weak Description: Block of text, hidden specifications, no brand voice.

Result: Low click-through rate, high bounce rate, lost sales.

After: The Engineered Listing

✓

Keyword Research & SEO Title: Data-driven keywords targeting high-intent search queries.

✓

AI Enhanced Images: Upscaled, background-removed, and placed into premium lifestyle mockups.

✓

Visual Hierarchy: Bulleted benefits, clear specifications, and trust-building copy.

✓

Result: Improved CTR, higher conversion rate, stronger algorithmic ranking.
